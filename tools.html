How these are documented

Every project follows the same two-part structure, because a tool without context is just a widget and an explanation without evidence is just a claim.

  1. Evidence - the artefact itself, embedded and usable: a live tool, a dataset, a drawing set or a test result.
  2. Explanation - the brief, the approach, the engineering reasoning, the validation plan and an honest account of what the work does not cover.
