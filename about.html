A little about me

I'm an engineering student at RMIT University who enjoys understanding how mechanical, electrical, and control systems fit together and bringing ideas to life. This portfolio brings together interactive engineering tools developed from my own hand/spreadsheet calculations alongside learnings from my uni studies and personal builds to demonstrate repeatability and systems engineering principles.

Additionally, I have a strong interest in how emerging workflows (such as AI-assisted coding and automated spreadsheet/SVG tools) can help engineers iterate faster when validated against real-world physics and first-principles maths.

When I'm not working on engineering projects, you can find me watching AFL football, catching up with friends, reading on my Kindle, or training in the gym.

What I enjoy working on

Design & analysis

Taking a project from a rough concept sketch through kinematic/stress analysis and CAD modelling to a tangible, working solution is what I find most rewarding.

Building & testing

Building and testing physical hardware makes a big difference in seeing where theoretical models hold up and where real-world friction or tolerances take over. For instance, in my robotic gripper project, each sensor/actuator had to be calibrated and verified individually before full system integration in LabVIEW.

Tools & technology

I predominantly use SolidWorks for 3D CAD modelling and technical drawings, whilst also having hands-on experience in Onshape, CATIA, Fusion 360, and AutoCAD. For analysis and control, I use MATLAB/Simulink for dynamic simulations and LabVIEW/Arduino for data acquisition and motor control. Outside of dedicated engineering packages, I enjoy building automated Excel spreadsheets, which directly inspired the interactive JavaScript/SVG calculators on this site.
