Links & joints

The actuator chosen for joint Ji+1 is physically mounted at the distal end of link i, so its mass is added there automatically. The base actuator (J1) is carried by the base and does not load the structure.

End effector

Total end-effector mass is applied as the distal lumped mass on the final link. The payload is separate - it is the object being gripped.

Design advice

    Formulas used (plain text) 
    SECTION      A, I about bending axis, c = extreme fibre, Z = I/c
MASS         m_i = A_i * L_i * 1e-9 * rho_i                      [kg]
COMPONENTS   actuator mass at joint Ji sits at distal end of link i-1
             P_i (distal lump of link i) = mass(act J_i+1)*qty + bracket
             P_N (last link)             = EE servo*qty + structure + tooling
REACH        S_i = sum of L_j for j >= i                          [mm]
PAYLOAD ARM  a_i = S_i + cg_offset                                [mm]
SELF WEIGHT  Sig_i = SUM_j>=i [ m_j*(S_i-S_j+L_j/2) + P_j*(S_i-S_j+L_j) ]
DEMAND       M_i = Kd * g * ( Payload*a_i + Sig_i )               [N.mm]
CAPACITY     V_i = Z_i * yield_i * k / FoS         (bending)      [N.mm]
             W_i = T_rated_i * qty_i * derate * ratio_i * eta * 1000
             X_i = MIN(V_i, W_i)
PAYLOAD      P_i = ( X_i/(Kd*g) - Sig_i ) / a_i , limit = MIN_i   [kg]
DEFLECTION   F_i  = Kd*g*(Payload + P_i + sum of distal m,P)      [N]
             Me_i = Kd*g*(Payload*a_i+1 + Sig_i+1)                [N.mm]
             w_i  = Kd*g*m_i / L_i                                [N/mm]
             th_i = (F L^2/2 + Me L + w L^3/6)/(E I)              [rad]
             d_i  = (F L^3/3 + Me L^2/2 + w L^4/8)/(E I)          [mm]
             droop = SUM d_i + SUM th_i * S_i+1
SERVO TORQUE T[N.m] = T[kg.cm] * 9.80665 / 100
SERVO SPEED  no-load deg/s = 60 / t60 (expect 50-70% under load)
DYNAMICS     J_i = SUM_j>=i [ m_j(L_j^2/12 + dcg^2) + P_j*dl^2 ] + Payload*a_i^2
             T_req = g*(Payload*a_i+Sig_i)/1000 + J_i*alpha       [N.m]
FREQUENCY    f_n ~ (1/2pi) * sqrt(3 E I / (m L^3))                [Hz]
    Disclaimer. Engineering aid based on idealised Euler–Bernoulli beam theory, a fully extended horizontal worst-case pose and indicative material properties. It ignores stress concentrations, joint/fastener detail, fatigue, creep, thermal softening and torsion. Verify any load-bearing design with FEA and physical testing. Printed-material figures are in-plane (XY) values - calibrate the knockdown factor k against your own test prints.

    Actuator data. Servo and stepper figures are indicative values for the common clone/class, compiled for comparison only - individual units vary widely between sellers. Hobby servo “kg·cm” ratings are stall torque at the quoted voltage: the motor is drawing maximum current, producing no useful motion and heating rapidly. Never size a joint at stall. Always confirm against the datasheet for the exact part you buy, and treat the derate factor as the single most important number on this page.
