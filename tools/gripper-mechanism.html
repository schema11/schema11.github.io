How the motion propagates

  1. Actuator → screw. The actuator rotates the axially located lead screw.
  2. Screw → nut. The nut translates by one screw lead per revolution.
  3. Nut → arm 1. The nut pin follows the arm-1 slot as the bell-crank turns.
  4. Arm 1 → arm 2. The arms form one rigid bell-crank with included angle β.
  5. Arm 2 → jaw. The second slot drives a slider bearing attached to the rail-mounted jaw carriage.
  6. Mirrored pair. Both jaws move symmetrically about the screw axis.

The jaw fingers and workpiece are on the far side of the linear rail from the motor. Verify slot reach and clearances against the SolidWorks assembly.
