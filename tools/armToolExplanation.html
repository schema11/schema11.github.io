1. Modelling approach

Each joint carries the full weight and moment of everything outboard of it. The model evaluates the worst-case pose (fully extended horizontally) against three independent checks: bending stress, actuator torque, and tip deflection. The smallest limit governs (stiffness usually fails first on printed arms).

$$M_i = K_d\,g\left[P\,S_i + \sum_{j \ge i}\left(m_j\left(S_i-S_j+\tfrac{L_j}{2}\right) + P_j\left(S_i-S_j+L_j\right)\right)\right]$$

$$M_{cap,i} = \min\!\left(Z_i\,\frac{\sigma_{y,i}\,k}{\text{FoS}},\ T_{motor,i}\cdot i_{gear}\cdot \eta\right) \qquad P_{max,i} = \frac{M_{cap,i}/(K_d\,g) - \Sigma_i}{S_i}$$

flowchart TD A[Geometry, material, motors] --> B[Section properties A, I, c, Z] B --> C[Link masses & reach to tip] C --> D[Self-weight moment per joint] D --> E[Governing capacity = min of bending & torque] E --> F[Payload limit = min across joints] F --> G{Tip deflection within budget?} G -- Yes --> H[Usable payload confirmed] G -- No --> I[Stiffen section, shorten link, or cut payload] I --> B

7. Additional engineering checks

  • Dynamics: \(J = \tfrac{1}{3}mL^2 + \sum m_{distal}d^2\), \(T_{req} = T_{gravity} + J\alpha + T_{friction}\). Check RMS torque against continuous ratings.
  • Natural frequency: \(f_n \approx \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{3EI}{mL^3}}\). Keep commanded motion below ~0.2 fn.
  • Buckling & torsion: \(P_{cr} = \pi^2 EI / (KL)^2\) for compression linkages, \(\tau = Tr / J_p\) during base slewing.
  • FDM practice: print links flat along their length with 4+ perimeters, derate PLA to ~40% for held poses (or use PETG/ASA), and use heat-set inserts at joints.
  • Accuracy & safety: include gearbox backlash, payload CoG overhang, E-stop, and power-off holding behaviour.

8. Validation checklist

Dead-weight test

Hang known masses in the horizontal pose and back-calculate real k from dial-indicator droop.

Joint FEA

Check bolt holes, fillets, and bearing seats where beam theory ignores stress concentrations.

Thermal & creep check

Hold a loaded pose for one hour and check temperature beside motors against Tg.
